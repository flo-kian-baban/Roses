Spring — S1 to S10 (new, 2026-10-11)

Pick 5 to 7, any mix of blossoms (S1–S5), petals (S6–S8) and buds (S9, S10), and reply with the numbers. The picks become Senso's spring scene, built like the fall and winter artwork, and the spring versions of the ten transitions (T1–T10) are then built from them.

Motion (the demo below and each card's middle pane): spring falls lighter than the leaves: slower, with a wider sway. A whole blossom twirls slowly as it falls (a full turn every 7–15 s, either way) and tilts a little; a single petal flips and floats (it tilts far over in 3D, like a petal in the air); a bud is heavier, drops a quarter faster and barely sways. Same rules as fall and winter: 20 particles (2 near, 9 middle, 9 far), random per load, full on the first frame, transform and opacity only; "Still" shows the blossoms resting low and along the sides.

Colours: natural palettes, like the leaves: pinks, white, violet-blue, gold, scarlet; the rosebud is Senso's own rose (#cc3366, the site pink the spring scene uses today). By analogy with the fall leaves and the summer sun (the PM, 2026-10-09), the picked artwork would carry its own colours, so the Style tab's "Spring blossoms" token would leave Senso's Welcome group (Kebab Land and the default template keep it). White petals carry a thin warm edge so they read on the cream.

Sizes: each design alone 0.7 KB–1.7 KB gzipped; all ten 9.6 KB. Six picks fit the 15 KB inline budget with room (fall's five leaves ship at 7.1 KB with the engine).
