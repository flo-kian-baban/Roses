Summer welcome candidates — Senso

Six ideas for the summer scene behind the welcome card, replacing the soft blurred light of 2026-10-09. Each phone runs live; pick one (or name a mix, for example S3's fruit with S4's bubbles) and reply with its number. Built 2026-10-10 by reports/welcome/summer/build.mjs (the artwork in art.mjs). Our tool, not a public page.

Same rules as fall and winter: original drawings (inline SVG, nothing traced or stock), at most 20 moving elements, a new random scene on every load and on Replay, already in motion on the first frame, movement on transform and opacity only, no blur anywhere. Still shows what a phone with reduced motion gets. Senso's cream background and card, as served.

Scope: Senso only, as with the leaves and snow; Kebab Land and the default template keep their summer scene until you confirm Senso's, then each gets its own in its own brand. Nothing on the customers' pages changes until you pick.
