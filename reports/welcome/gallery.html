Welcome artwork candidates — Senso

Ten fall leaves (F1–F10) and ten snowflakes (W1–W10), all original inline SVG, each defined once as a symbol. Pick 5 to 7 per season and reply with the numbers. Built 2026-10-09 by reports/welcome/gallery/build.mjs (the artwork in art.mjs). Our tool, not a public page.

How to read a card: the design large and still; the same design falling on its own with the step-2 motion (near layer); the design at the far, middle and near sizes on Senso's welcome background (cream #fff8ee). Under each season, the full-scene demo shows all ten together on a replica of Senso's welcome screen; Replay draws a new random scene, Still shows the reduced-motion composition.

Colours: the leaves carry their own natural palettes (orange, rust, gold, red, brown, two-tone turning); they are not tinted by the Style tab's "Fall leaves" token (open question in the reply). The snow is drawn in the "Winter snow" token colour, which is #b2becc on Senso's cream (the Auto rule); W2 and W4 carry white plates.
