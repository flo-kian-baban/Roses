Run on 046a2ba (the version the owner confirmed) against a scratch copy of the live data; method and limits in [method.md](method.md), the measuring script [ui-audit.mjs](ui-audit.mjs), the numbers in [data/](data/), the stills in `reports/ui-audit/shots/` on Kian's laptop only (gitignored: they show photos and phone numbers). **Checked and fine**: no sideways scrolling or clipped headings on any of the 32 walks; no script or page errors; every text colour measured passes WCAG AA (3,084 measurements of visible text over its background across the walks' steps, 0 below 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text; text over photos and the welcome artwork not measured); right-to-left mirroring of the header, tabs, list icon, popup and section list (✕ and Close on the start side in both languages); the tab underline follows the scroll and every section jump (heading lands under the tab bar); Escape and the ✕ close the popup; reduced motion (still scene, no animation running, the menu in 188 ms on Senso and 50 ms on Kebab Land, the popup in place at 120 ms); slow Slow-4G placeholders (grey squares, no layout jumps; the photos themselves are slow, the known "after-tap photos" miss); staff wording for the price rule ("The item needs a price before it can be shown"), an empty search ("Nothing matches. Show all"), allergens ("Allergens and diet: set by the owner or an admin."), a wrong PIN ("wrong PIN (4 tries left)").

What people see today (nothing is hosted, so no app could fetch it; this is what WhatsApp and iMessage do with these tags): WhatsApp, the title, the description and the address, no image; iMessage, the title on a plain card, with the logo as a small icon at best (Kebab Land's is invisible on the light card).

Proposed per venue (stills `share-proposal-senso.png`, `share-proposal-kebab-land.png`, 1200 × 630): the logo on the venue's own background (Senso cream, Kebab Land its recorded dark tile), "Menu · منو" and one line ("Persian breakfast, coffee, desserts · Yonge St, Richmond Hill" / "Persian grill and full bar · Yonge St, Richmond Hill"). No food photos (the owner's written permission for photos is still pending, and a photo would go stale with the season). The vector logos the client still owes would make them sharper. The tags need the final domain (absolute URLs), so they belong with the hosting step; the images can be made now.
